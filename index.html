How this is calculated
  • Annual hours saved = people × hours saved per week × working weeks × adoption rate
  • Annual gross savings = annual hours saved × hourly cost
  • First-year net benefit = annual gross savings − annual operating cost − one-time implementation cost
  • Payback time = implementation cost ÷ (annual gross savings − annual operating cost), shown in months. If annual gross savings do not exceed the annual operating cost, there is no payback under these assumptions.

Empty or negative inputs are treated as 0. The adoption rate is capped at 100%. Amounts are shown in Moroccan dirham (MAD), rounded to the nearest dirham.
